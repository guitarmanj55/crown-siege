Crown Siege

Hold the keep. Topple the dark tower.

How to play

  • Miners dig gold and tend the crystal for mana. More miners, more gold. Miners and soldiers share one population of 40; tap the miner button with the red − to send one home (refunds 25 gold) and free a place for a soldier.
  • Footmen are cheap. Archers shoot from behind. Shield knights soak blows and cost mana. Battle wizards hurl fire orbs that ignore armour and burst on groups; they are fragile, so keep them behind the line.
  • Long stalemates end: after eight minutes the walls crack and buildings take more and more damage.
  • Garrison pulls the army into the keep (it heals there, and adds arrows). Defend holds the line. Attack marches on the tower.
  • ⚡ Lightning: tap it, then tap the battlefield. Costs mana, then recharges.
  • Drag to look around; tap the minimap to jump.
